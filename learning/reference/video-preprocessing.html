FSL training lab / Field notes

Video → landmarks → dataset.

Input contract

  • One complete labeled gesture per clip; trim unrelated footage yourself.
  • Constant frame rate, readable by OpenCV, at least two decoded frames.
  • Extensions: .mp4, .avi, .mov, .mkv, .webm, .m4v.
  • At least two classes and three independent accepted source groups per class for preparation.
  • Keep related clips/session/signer groups together with explicit IDs when required.
data/<category>/<gesture>/recording.mp4
python categories.py prepare --category practice_video --datasets datasets_video_v1
python trainer.py inspect --dataset datasets_video_v1/practice_video

Run commands from the repository root with the project's ML Python environment. Choose an unused dataset output.

What happens automatically

Every frame is decoded, converted BGR → RGB, and sent to a fresh MediaPipe VIDEO detector for that clip. Up to two hands supply 128 values per frame: left XYZ (63), right XYZ (63), and two presence flags. Missing hands are zeros.

Extraction saves (T,128). Preparation assigns source groups to separate splits, then interpolates coordinates to 32 timesteps and uses nearest-frame presence flags. Every video yields one (32,128) sample; static jitter variants do not apply to videos. No gesture segmentation, extra centering, or hand-size normalization is performed.

A video with sparse detection can pass acceptance. Inspect low detected_frames / frames ratios. Failure reports are written beside the requested dataset folder.
